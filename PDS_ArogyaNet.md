# Product Definition Sheet (PDS)
## ArogyaNet

---

**Tagline:** *"See the shortage before it happens. Move resources before it's a crisis."*

**Track:** Track 3 — Smart Health & Supply Chain Resilience (BRICS Theme: Resilience)

---

### The Problem (one line)
Local health centres run out of medicine and staff unpredictably because no one has a live, network-wide view of who has what.

### The Solution (one line)
An AI dashboard that watches every health centre's stock, beds, and staff in real time — and tells administrators exactly what will run out, and exactly where to pull resources from to fix it.

### Who It's For
District and state-level public health administrators managing networks of Primary Health Centres (PHCs).

### Key Features
- 🗺️ **Live network dashboard** — color-coded status of every PHC at a glance
- 🔍 **Drill-down view** — item-level stock, bed occupancy, staff attendance per centre
- ⚠️ **AI early warnings** — plain-language alerts before stock-outs happen
- 🔄 **AI redistribution recommendations** — "move X units from A to B" with reasoning
- 📈 **Simple demand forecasting** — trend-based stock-out predictions

### What Makes It Different
Most inventory dashboards just show numbers. ArogyaNet uses AI to reason across the *entire network at once* and tells the administrator the specific action to take — not just the problem, but the fix.

### Tech at a Glance
Gemini API (reasoning + natural language alerts) + a simple web dashboard + real government health data (where available) blended with realistic synthetic data.

### BRICS Angle
Built as a Digital Public Good: the data model and AI logic are designed to be reusable across any BRICS nation's public health network, enabling shared predictive models and coordinated resilience.

### One-Sentence Pitch for Judges
*"ArogyaNet turns a fragmented network of health centres into one intelligent system that predicts shortages and tells you exactly how to fix them — before patients ever feel the gap."*
